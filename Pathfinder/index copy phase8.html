Build sequence
    Explainable scoring

    How Pathfinder scores you

    01 — Career fit

    Fit combines your questionnaire signals across every available track. The winner is the track with the strongest weighted preference signal.

    02 — Readiness

    Readiness is based on the skills Pathfinder can find in your pasted experience. Missing evidence is treated conservatively instead of assuming you know a skill.

    03 — Confidence

    Confidence rises when your career preference and skill evidence point in the same direction. This makes the recommendation easier to explain to judges and users.
